Required Qualifications

Core educational milestones generally expected across clinical and specialized paths.

  • check_circle

    Bachelor's Degree

    In Nutrition, Dietetics, or a related science field from an accredited institution.

  • check_circle

    Supervised Practice Program

    Completion of an ACEND-accredited dietetic internship or coordinated program.

  • check_circle

    Master's Degree (Upcoming Requirement)

    Required for CDR registration eligibility starting in 2024 in many regions.

Key Certifications

Professional credentials that establish authority and specific scopes of practice.

  • workspace_premium

    Registered Dietitian (RD/RDN)

    The foundational credential for clinical practice, awarded by the CDR.

  • workspace_premium

    Certified Nutrition Specialist (CNS)

    Advanced certification for personalized nutrition professionals.

  • workspace_premium

    Board Certified Specialist (CSSD, CSG, etc.)

    Specialized credentials in sports, gerontology, pediatrics, or oncology.
